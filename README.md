# Harun's Scholarly Hub

Build a comprehensive, modern, and fully responsive academic portfolio and bio-data website for an English Literature scholar. You are acting as an expert React developer and UI/UX designer. Use standard UI components and Tailwind CSS to create a polished, scholarly aesthetic.

*Design System & Aesthetic:*

* *Typography:* Use a classic, elegant Serif font (like Merriweather or Playfair Display) for headings to give a literary feel, and a highly readable Sans-Serif (like Inter or Open Sans) for body text.

* *Colors:* Muted, professional academic tones—deep navy blue, charcoal grey, and soft off-white or parchment backgrounds. Avoid bright or neon colors.

* *Layout:* A clean navigation bar at the top (sticky) that links to distinct sections. The layout should be highly readable on both desktop and mobile.

*Please include the following sections and inject this exact content:*

*1. Hero / Home Section*

* *Headline:* Harun Chaudhari

* *Sub-headline:* Ph.D. Candidate in English Literature & Academic Researcher

* *Call to Action Buttons:* "View Research" (scrolls to research) and "Download Full CV" (placeholder download button).

*2. About & Bio-Data Section*

* Create a clean, grid-based bio-data card containing:

* *Age:* 33

* *Marital Status:* Married

* *Core Qualification:* GSET (Gujarat State Eligibility Test) Cleared

* Add a brief introductory paragraph about being a dedicated literature scholar focusing on 17th-century prose.

*3. Research & Scholarship Section*

* Create a prominent featured card for current doctoral research.

* *Dissertation Title:* "Logic and mysticism in the selected works of Thomas Browne"

* *Description:* Add placeholder text explaining the academic analysis of 17th-century prose, treatises, and essays.

*4. Teaching & Professional Experience Section*

* *Previous Role:* Assistant Professor of English at Shree Morarji Desai Arts & Com. College, Buhari.

* *Pedagogical Expertise:* Highlight experience developing higher education curricula, study guides, and multiple-choice questions.

* Use tags or chips to list subject areas: Environmental Literature, Literary Theory, English Grammar.

*5. Resources & Portfolio Section*

* Create a list or grid of downloadable resources with document icons where users could theoretically download syllabus materials, study guides, or research notes.

*6. Contact & Footer*

* A clean, functional-looking contact form (Name, Email, Message, Submit).

* A footer with copyright details and placeholder links for academic networks (e.g., LinkedIn, Google Scholar).

Ensure the generated application is interactive, features smooth scrolling between sections, and looks like a premium, finished academic website ready for deployment.
